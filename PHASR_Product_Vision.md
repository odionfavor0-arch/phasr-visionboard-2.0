# PHASR — Product Vision Document

**Created:** March 2026
**Last updated:** June 2026

---

## What is PHASR?

PHASR is an action-driven vision board ecosystem built for people, primarily women, who deeply want to achieve their goals but lack a system that removes friction and keeps them accountable all year round. It bridges the gap between dreaming and doing.

---

## The Problem

Vision boards exist everywhere. Every January, women create them. By March, they're forgotten. There is no structured, emotional, community-backed system that takes someone from visualization all the way through to execution, and keeps bringing them back.

PHASR solves that.

---

## Who PHASR Is For

She has tried every manifestation and self-improvement thing in the book. Daily affirmations, meditation, vision board prayer, manifestation rituals. She is not undisciplined, she is over-equipped with tools that don't talk to each other. She does a lot, and somehow still ends up with nothing to show for the year.

She has never known what it feels like to follow through on a plan. That is not a feature gap. That is an identity wound. She doesn't just need a task tracker, she needs proof that she is capable of finishing what she starts.

She is both emotional and ambitious, and needs a system that honors both. She may have ADHD or focus challenges, meaning she needs friction removed between her and action, not more steps added.

She has tried all of this alone. Part of why none of it stuck is that no one was watching, no one held her accountable, no one was doing the work alongside her.

## Core Design Principles

**Remove every friction between the user and the action.** If the system makes it easier to do the thing than to avoid it, people will show up.

**Match the user's actual thought pattern in real time, don't just clear a fixed path.** "Remove friction" is too generic. The sharper target: a user with ADHD doesn't need fewer steps, she needs a system that can hold five open threads the way her head does, without forcing her to close one before starting another. This is the difference between "make it simple" and "make it elastic enough to fit how she actually thinks." It is the reason the product feels addictive in the good sense, not a dopamine loop, but a "this finally works the way I work" recognition. That retention can't be faked with onboarding flows or streaks. It comes from matching cognition, not just behavior.

**Stay present in a person's life without demanding attention.** PHASR should not only exist when the app is open. The danger with most productivity tools is that intention dies the moment the app closes. PHASR should be able to exist quietly in the periphery, a streak number, a small nudge from Sage, a single word of encouragement, so the user is gently pulled back toward one small action even mid-scroll, mid-distraction, mid-life. (Future direction: iOS Live Activity / Dynamic Island presence so Sage can stay visible even while the user is in another app.)

**Community is the quiet backbone, not the headline feature.** Like a blog underneath a business, community in PHASR isn't the first thing she sees, it's the infrastructure that makes everything else hold. It exists to support the real goal: proving to herself she can follow through.

**The platform should feel dreamy, not just functional.** She can come, dream, plan, and that plan should not stay theoretical. Immediately after creating a vision board or a plan, she should be able to see a path into real life. Even if someone drops off for a while, when they come back, the system should meet them exactly where they are and say: just one thing today. Momentum over completion.

## Core Features

### 1. Vision Board with Pillars
- Set 3 to 4 goals per year
- Break each goal into phases with specific timelines
- Each phase has 1 to 2 focus pillars
- Upload before and after photos per pillar
- AI generates resources, weekly non-negotiables, and daily activities per pillar
- Each pillar is saveable as a shareable image with QR code linking back to personal dashboard

### 2. Daily Streak System
- Goals stretched across weeks and months
- Daily task and to-do list per week
- Dynamic content rotates every 24 hours
- Streaks unlock ranks and features the more consistently you show up

### 3. Weekly Reset with Sage
- At the end of each week, user does a reflection session with Sage
- Sage asks guided questions
- Based on answers, Sage designs the next 7 days
- Keeps momentum even when life disrupts the plan

### 4. Sage — The AI
- Floating AI assistant woven throughout the entire app
- Guides every step of the journey
- Generates action steps, resources, and templates per goal type
- Examples: savings spreadsheet for finance goals, job hunt tracker for career goals
- Scrapes relevant sources and distills them into one ready-to-use template
- Removes every possible friction between the user and the action

### 5. Community
- Chat rooms matched by similar goals
- Users can find accountability partners
- Comment, hype, and check in on each other
- Live rooms for real-time co-working or goal sessions
- Physical cohorts for deeper community

### 6. Journaling Section
- Free write or use guided templates
- Templates for general goals, mental health, career, finances, and more
- Sage reads and responds, users can process with Sage directly from their journal
- Designed for emotional processing alongside structured achievement

### 7. Statistics Dashboard
- Visual progress tracking across all pillars and phases
- Shows consistency, streaks, and growth over time
